While you study, build

Certificates prove what you know. A project proves what you can build.

The world is about to be run by AI workers, and very few people can build them safely. You can be one of them.

You would not hand a new accounts clerk the bank password and say "pay whatever looks right." AI workers need the same care, and almost nobody builds that part. That is DSoR, the Data System of Record: the checkpoint an AI worker passes before it touches a payment, a customer record or an order.

KSoR · KnowledgeThe rulebookWhat an AI worker may know.
DSoR · DataThe checkpointWhat an AI worker may do.

From the day you pass PCAO-F, you build DSoR with us in 52 baby steps, using Claude Code. Step 00 is live and assumes you know none of this.

Core concept

What is a Digital FTE?

Digital FTEs, also called Digital Workers, are reliable AI agents that perform structured knowledge work continuously inside real organizations. Not just a model with a prompt. A system.

Four parts of one system

Hover a node to follow its signal into the Digital FTE.

Domain Expertise
Explicit Specifications
Engineering Architecture
Human Oversight

Not automation. Scalable assets.

Digital FTEs are an economic construct as well as a technical one. They let organizations package expertise, reduce execution bottlenecks and create new revenue streams. Built well, they become scalable assets.
