# Panaversity homepage sample

An **unofficial replica / concept redesign** of the [panaversity.org](https://panaversity.org/) homepage, made for visual and design review only.

This project is **not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by Panaversity**. Panaversity, PIAIC, GIAIC and Operation Badar names, logos, photos and text belong to their respective owners. Courses, enrolment, exams and learner accounts exist only on the official website: <https://panaversity.org/>.

## What is in here

- `index.html`: the whole page (HTML, CSS and a little vanilla JS). Open it in a browser, or serve the folder (for example `python3 -m http.server`). It follows the light/dark preference and opens with a notice that it is a replica.
- `images/`: the original homepage images (logos, partner logos, faculty and testimonial photos, class thumbnails, section background and hero video)
- `svgs/`: the two redesigned animated SVGs used on the page
  - `career-orbit.svg`: the career-path orbit
  - `fte-hub.svg`: the Digital FTE hub

## What it covers

Every section of the original homepage: hero, career-ready path, build-with-DSoR (with an interactive four-check demo), curriculum (all five courses), free preview, faculty, Zia Tutor AI, Digital FTE and the 10-80-10 rhythm, about and stats, ecosystem, testimonials, the complete FAQ (all four tabs) and the footer. It also adds a Claude Certification Pathway section built from the site's own FAQ.

Colours and fonts follow the current panaversity.org brand (Poppins and Rubik, `#1cd98e` green, `#050316` ink, `#031811` deep green).
